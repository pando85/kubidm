# Artwork and brand provenance

The current Kubidm product identity is **T1.1 Balanced**, documented in [brand/README.md](brand/README.md). Canonical, independently generated source vectors are under `artwork/brand/`. Historical logo files remain in place for attribution and archive purposes; please do not use the older shield/key artwork for new interfaces.

## Historical artwork

The original Kanidm-related artwork in this repository includes contributions from:
- Jesse Irwin (@wizardfortress), commissioned artwork;
- @ateneatla, Christmas artwork;
- Pi-Cla, recursive logo.

We thank those artists and the original Kanidm maintainers. The historical artwork is documented as **CC-BY-NC-ND**; its licensing and attribution are not changed by the new Kubidm brand assets. Do not assume that this historical license applies to the newly created T1.1 identity.
